# Prompt Playbook — the free app

A Jade Static Builds project, built with Muse.

Learn the tiers of prompting in plain words, then build a ready prompt you can paste into any AI chat. The app adds the parts a good prompt needs — a goal, limits, a check step, and a stop rule — and explains which tier your prompt is and how to lift it one tier higher.

**Live:** https://jadestaticbuilds.github.io/prompt-playbook/

## What it does

- **The tiers, in plain words** — Tier 1 (just ask) through Tier 4 (run it on a clock), with a short note on Tiers 5–6, each with an example you can try in the builder.
- **Prompt builder** — type an idea (or tap one of 12 starter ideas), pick a tier, add optional details (limits that matter, must-never rules, what done looks like), and get a complete prompt with category-matched check and stop lines. Shopping, research, and money prompts get their own safety shapes: verified prices, cited sources, no buying or moving money without your yes.
- **Prompt check** — every result shows which of Goal / Limits / Check / Stop are covered, and what is missing. A completeness check, not a quality score.
- **Shorter / Longer** — trim a prompt without cutting its stop or approval lines, or strengthen it with clarify and success lines.
- **Saved prompts** — save prompts in your browser, with titles, dates, search, and collapse/expand, plus Export/Import as JSON. Nothing is uploaded: no account, no server, prompts never leave your browser. Anonymous visit counts (GoatCounter) never include prompt content.
- **A taste of the system** — a short governance section (gates and stop rules), a filled delegation-contract example, and three decision prompts. The full method — the complete governance chapter, a worked before/after library, all ten decision prompts taught, worksheets, and a 7-day setup plan — is the paid edition, in preparation.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages site above. Single file, no build step, no server needed.
